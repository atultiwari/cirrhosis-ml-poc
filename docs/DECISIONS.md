## Phase 2 — Track A models (2026-11-03)
- 2026-11-03 — Sample size: own Python implementation of Riley et al. survival criteria (Stat Med 2019; BMJ 2020), verified against the BMJ 2020 worked example (n = 5143, 692 events, EPP 23.07) — Atul prefers Python over R; PyPI `pmsampsize` needs Python ≥3.12 — R `pmsampsize` (rejected: no R). Anticipated R²_CS: optimistic = 1−exp(−199.2/312) from Dickson's initial-model LR χ²; conservative = 15% of max R²_CS (Riley default).
- 2026-11-03 — Fair Mayo comparator (Atul's choice): Cox on the 5 Mayo variables (log bilirubin, log albumin, log PT, age, edema) refitted inside every CV training fold is the primary benchmark; the published fixed score is shown alongside, labelled optimistic. Check: the refit on all 312 reproduces Dickson's initial-model coefficients to 3 decimals.
- 2026-11-03 — Candidate predictors: 16 baseline variables (8 skewed labs log-transformed; stage as linear 1–4; binary signs 0/1); treatment arm excluded (no effect in the trial; undefined for non-trial patients). Stage (needs biopsy) kept because it is a recorded baseline variable; flagged for the RNT schema.
- 2026-11-03 — Missing data: IterativeImputer (chained equations, single deterministic imputation (posterior means), max_iter 10) inside the training fold, after a stateless log transform — avoids leakage; full MI with Rubin's rules judged unnecessary for the 312 primary set (≤10% missing per variable) but is a limitation for the 418 sensitivity analysis (106 patients missing all of copper/cholesterol/ALP/AST/triglycerides).
- 2026-11-03 — Models: Cox (ridge 1e-4 for numerical stability), elastic-net Cox (l1_ratio {0.1,0.5,0.9} × 8 alphas 0.001–0.32), RSF (300 trees; min_samples_leaf {5,10,20} × max_features {sqrt, 0.5}), gradient-boosted Cox (lr 0.05, subsample 0.8; n_estimators {100,300} × depth {1,2}). DeepSurv not attempted (n≈300).
- 2026-11-03 — Validation: repeated nested CV, outer 5-fold × 10 repeats stratified on death, inner 3-fold KFold grid search scored by Harrell's C (sksurv default). IPCW metrics in each fold use the outer training fold for the censoring distribution. Inner 3 folds (not 5) to keep ~60 events per inner training split.
- 2026-11-03 — Reported performance: per-patient OOF 5-y risk averaged over the 10 repeats, then C / Uno C / td-AUC with 1000 bootstrap CIs, calibration and DCA on these OOF risks; fold-level mean (SD) also reported. Caveat: bootstrap of OOF predictions ignores refitting variability (CIs somewhat narrow). Discrimination ranks patients by 5-y risk (identical to the linear predictor for Cox models).
- 2026-11-03 — IBS over 1–7 y on the integer grid — the published Mayo S0(t) exists only for 1–7 y, so all models share that window.
- 2026-11-03 — Calibration at 5 y: decile groups vs KM observed risk; O/E ratio; calibration slope = Cox regression of outcome (follow-up truncated at 5 y) on log(−log(1−p)). DCA at 5 y with KM-based net benefit (Vickers 2008), thresholds 0.05–0.60.
- 2026-11-03 — Explainability on final models refitted on all 312 (descriptive/apparent only): permutation importance (ΔHarrell C, 20 repeats) for Cox/elastic-net/RSF/GBSA; model-agnostic permutation SHAP (50-patient background) on the risk score for RSF and GBSA, in raw predictor units; SHAP dependence plots replace partial-dependence plots for bilirubin, albumin, PT and age.
- 2026-11-03 — PH assumption: Schoenfeld tests (rank time) reported; log PT shows non-proportionality (p≈0.02), consistent with Dickson's note on time-varying effects — kept as is for comparability; tree models do not assume PH.
- 2026-11-03 — Sensitivity analyses with 5 CV repeats each (compute budget): (a) all 418 patients, imputation inside folds; (b) composite endpoint death or transplant; (c) transplant as competing event, described with Aalen–Johansen CIF and decile calibration vs AJ — Fine–Gray not fitted (no mature Python implementation; would need R `cmprsk`/`riskRegression`).
- 2026-11-03 — After code review: per-repeat results (each repeat = one honest CV pass) reported as mean (range) next to the repeat-averaged OOF analysis, because averaging over repeats behaves like a small ensemble — alternative: whole-pipeline bootstrap (≈1000× compute; rejected for a PoC).
- 2026-11-03 — DCA net benefit and grouped KM calibration are set to NaN (not extrapolated) when a subset has < 10 patients or its follow-up ends before 5 y — avoids KM carry-forward artefacts and matches the n < 10 suppression rule.
- 2026-11-03 — Paired model-vs-benchmark differences are descriptive and unadjusted for multiplicity (5 models × 5 metrics); stated in the report.
- 2026-11-03 — Imputation model excludes the outcome (event, time) — test-time imputation must not use the outcome, and this mirrors deployment; known to attenuate imputed-predictor associations slightly — alternative: outcome in training-fold imputation only (asymmetric; rejected for simplicity).
- 2026-11-03 — Aalen–Johansen tie-jitter checked: 5-y death CIF identical to 4 decimals across 10 jitter seeds (0.2837).
- 2026-11-03 — Sample-size output enforces Riley criteria 1–2; criterion 3 (precision of overall 5-y risk) is reported, not enforced (as in pmsampsize survival). EPP for forests/boosting is optimistic (more effective parameters than candidate predictors).
